Experiment 1: MOSFET & IGBT Characterization

V–I Characteristics • Switching Times • Switching Losses

1. Student Details

2. Aim

To study the V–I characteristics, switching times and switching losses of a power MOSFET and an IGBT experimentally.

3. Learning Objectives

  • Understand the operation of MOSFET and IGBT as power switches.
  • Determine the V–I characteristics.
  • Measure turn-on and turn-off switching times.
  • Determine switching energy and switching losses.
  • Compare the switching behaviour of MOSFET and IGBT.

5. Theory

MOSFET

A power MOSFET is a voltage-controlled semiconductor device. Its conduction is controlled by the gate-to-source voltage.

VGS → Gate control voltage
VDS → Drain-source voltage
ID → Drain current

IGBT

An IGBT is a voltage-controlled power semiconductor device combining MOS gate control with high current and voltage capability.

VGE → Gate-emitter voltage
VCE → Collector-emitter voltage
IC → Collector current

Switching Time

tON = td(ON) + tr tOFF = td(OFF) + tf

Switching Loss

p(t) = v(t) × i(t) Esw = ∫ v(t)i(t)dt Psw = fs × (EON + EOFF)

7. Procedure

1. Connect the MOSFET test circuit as per the circuit diagram.
2. Connect the resistive load to the power circuit.
3. Connect the gate driver to the MOSFET.
4. Apply the required DC supply.
5. Apply the gate pulse.
6. Measure VGS, VDS and ID using the oscilloscope.
7. Vary the gate voltage and record the corresponding drain current.
8. Determine turn-on and turn-off switching times.
9. Determine switching energy and switching losses.
10. Repeat the procedure for the IGBT.
11. Compare the MOSFET and IGBT characteristics.
